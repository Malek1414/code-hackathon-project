# FollowCam — 40-second spoken pitch (Malek)

~105 words. Speak briskly, pause at the beat marks. Hold the rig while
talking; play 03_combined_30s.mp4 muted behind you.

---

**[0–10s — what it is + hardware]**
This is FollowCam — the twenty-euro robot cameraman. Two 3D-printed parts
clamp onto any tripod: a ring on the column, a fork arm on a metal-gear
servo, driven by an Arduino.

**[10–25s — how it works]**
Your phone sits in the head. Our app tracks the ball on-device and steers
the servo — a hundred-degree sweep covers a full court from the scorer's
table. Every clip feeds our vision pipeline: player tracking, jersey
numbers, a 2D court model.

**[25–40s — the goal, the kicker]**
And from that: live score, points, rebounds, assists — even heart rate
synced to mistakes. Pivo follows a person. FollowCam films the game AND
keeps the stats. Twenty euros, not two thousand.
