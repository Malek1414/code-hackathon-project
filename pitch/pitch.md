# Pitch — FollowCam (rename with team)

## The 15-second recruit line (10:30 tomorrow)
"I'm building a robot cameraman: a 3D-printed hand that grips a normal tripod handle and follows the ball with AI. Hardware's designed, print files are ready. I need a vision person, a pitch/design person, and a builder — we demo it live on stage."

## Stage pitch structure (3–4 min, demo FIRST)

1. **Cold open — the demo (60s).** No slides. Ball walks across the room, camera follows. Then one line: "That tripod cost €30. The robot on it cost €20 to print. The thing it replaces costs €2,000."
2. **Problem (30s).** Millions of amateur and youth games are never filmed, never analyzed, never scored properly — because auto-tracking cameras (Veo, Pixellot) cost thousands and a human cameraman costs more. The games with the least support are the ones kids and volunteers run.
3. **Solution (30s).** Don't sell a camera. Retrofit the tripod they already own: printed clamp + hobby servo + their phone + our tracking software. Ball detection today; player tags (stick-on markers, any ball, any team) next.
4. **Platform (45s).** The follow-rig is the capture layer of a position-data platform: (a) broadcast overlays — names and stats above players, accessible viewing for kids and viewers with disabilities; (b) team analytics — movement, formations, opponent prediction; (c) roadmap: the camera that follows the ball can keep the score — auto-with-veto scorekeeping for volunteer-run leagues.
5. **Why us / today (20s).** Designed, printed, wired, and demoed in one day at CODE — initiative and community, from day one. Next: film a real game this semester.

## 60–90s video pitch script (film at 14:30, submit by 15:15)
- 0–10s: Hook shot — rig follows the ball, no narration, just the servo sound.
- 10–25s: One-liner + problem over b-roll of the print coming off the bed.
- 25–50s: How it works: phone screen showing tracking box → laptop → servo → handle.
- 50–75s: Platform slide (three surfaces) + price comparison.
- 75–90s: Team names + "built in 7 hours at CODE."

## Anticipated Q&A
- "What about tilt/vertical?" — Horizontal covers 90% of court sports framing; tilt is the same mechanism rotated, v2.
- "Why not just digital crop on a wide lens?" — We do that too (show fallback C if built); physical pan keeps full sensor resolution on the action, which analytics needs.
- "Tracking players without numbers?" — Stick-on tags, same as the ball. That's the product's data moat: cheap markers anyone can apply.
- "Privacy, filming kids?" — On-device processing, short retention; the score-tracker roadmap was designed around German gym privacy from day one.
